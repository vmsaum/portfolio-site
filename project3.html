Project 3 - Electric Vehicle Adoption Trends in Washington State

This project focused on analyzing electric vehicle adoption trends in Washington State using electric vehicle registration data. The goal was to identify adoption patterns, compare vehicle types, examine geographic trends, and better understand the factors influencing electric vehicle growth throughout the state.

The project was completed as part of a collaborative data science team. My role involved developing recommendations, addressing ethical considerations, writing the conclusion, organizing references, and contributing to the appendix. These responsibilities helped connect the analysis results to practical applications and future decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze electric vehicle adoption trends across Washington State
  • Compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)
  • Identify geographic patterns in electric vehicle ownership
  • Evaluate factors influencing electric vehicle adoption and growth

Tools & Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Google Colab
  • Pandas
  • LightGBM
  • Matplotlib
  • GitHub
  • PowerPoint

Key Findings

The analysis showed that electric vehicle adoption continues to grow throughout Washington State. Battery Electric Vehicles were more common than Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles, and urban counties demonstrated significantly higher adoption rates. The machine learning model achieved approximately 80% accuracy and helped identify important factors associated with electric vehicle adoption patterns.
