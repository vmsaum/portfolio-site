Project 1 - Crime Pattern Analysis in Los Angeles

This project focused on analyzing crime patterns in Los Angeles between 2020 and 2024 using data science methods and visualization techniques. The goal was to identify trends related to crime type, time of occurrence, geographic location, and victim demographics.

The project was completed as part of a collaborative data science team. My role involved data visualization and presentation development, where I helped organize findings into clear visual reports and presentation materials.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze crime trends across time periods
  • Identify geographic areas with higher crime rates
  • Examine victim demographics and age groups
  • Visualize patterns using charts and data analysis

Tools & Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Jupyter Notebook
  • Pandas
  • Matplotlib
  • GitHub
  • PowerPoint

Key Findings

The analysis showed that crime occurred most frequently during nighttime hours and was concentrated in specific geographic locations such as Central and 77th Street areas. Adults aged 50 and older appeared most frequently in reported incidents, while vehicle theft was identified as one of the most common crime categories.
